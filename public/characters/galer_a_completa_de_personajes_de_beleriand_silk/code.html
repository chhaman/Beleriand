"Que se guarden sus nombres en las piedras de Beleriand, pues sus destinos tejieron la música del mundo."

Legendary Figures

Fëanor

High King of the Noldor • Elf

The greatest of the Eldar in skill and word, creator of the Silmarils, whose oath led the Noldor into the shadows of Middle-earth.

Key Deed:

"Crafted the Silmarils, trapping the light of the Two Trees of Valinor."

Fingolfin

King of Hithlum • Elf

Most valiant of the kings of old, who rode alone to the gates of Angband to challenge the Dark Lord in single combat.

Key Deed:

"Wounded Morgoth seven times before the gates of hell."

Lúthien Tinúviel

Princess of Doriath • Half-Maia

The morning star of the Elves, who through song and love conquered the darkness of the Iron Crown.

Key Deed:

"Put Morgoth to sleep and recovered a Silmaril from his crown."

Beren

Outlaw of Dorthonion • Man

A mortal man who accomplished an impossible quest for the love of an immortal maiden, becoming a legend across ages.

Key Deed:

"Passed through the Girdle of Melian and breached the fortress of Angband."

Túrin Turambar

Master of Doom • Man

A hero of great strength and sorrow, cursed by the Dark Lord to bring ruin to all he loved, yet slayer of the Father of Dragons.

Key Deed:

"Slew Glaurung the Golden, the first of the Great Urulóki."

Morgoth Bauglir

The Great Enemy • Ainu

The first Dark Lord, who lusted for the light and wrought discord in the music of the world, ruling from the pits of Angband.

Key Deed:

"Destroyed the Two Trees and stole the Silmarils from Valinor."
