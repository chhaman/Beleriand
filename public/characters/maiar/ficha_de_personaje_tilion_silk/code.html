"He loved silver, and he loved the light of Telperion, often seeking its rays even when forbidden."

El Guardián de la Última Flor

Before the Moon was a vessel in the sky, it was the last fruit of the Silver Tree of Valinor. When the Great Trees were destroyed by Melkor and Ungoliant, Telperion bore one last bloom of silver radiance.

Tilion, who had long cherished the silver gleam of the Elder Tree, begged for the task of guiding this final flower. His love for the light was so great that he was granted the honor of piloting Isil, the silver sheen that would light the darkness of Middle-earth.

El legado de la Luz Nocturna

Tilion remains the eternal helmsman, a beacon of hope in the darkest of nights. Unlike Arien, the Maia of the Sun who is constant and blinding, Tilion is known for his erratic path, often drawn too close to the Sun in his admiration of its brilliance, causing the phases of the moon and eclipses.

The Lunar Path

His journey is not a fixed line but a dance across the sky, reflecting his untamed nature.

Friend of Stars

He works in harmony with the constellations placed by Varda, the Queen of Stars.
