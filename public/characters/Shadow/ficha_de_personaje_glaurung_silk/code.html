Biography

First fire dragon created by Morgoth Bauglir in the pits of Angband. Unlike his winged descendants, Glaurung was a massive flightless serpent of immense power and malevolence. He was not just a physical force of destruction, but a psychological terror expert, capable of weaving powerful enchantments with his gaze.
