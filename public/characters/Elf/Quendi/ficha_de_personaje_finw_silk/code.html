El Padre de las Casas Reales: Finwë

Rey Supremo de los Noldor en Aman • Elf (Noldor)

shield El Sacrificio en Formenos

Finwë was the first to fall in the Blessed Realm. Slain by Morgoth at the threshold of Formenos while defending the Silmarils, his death marked the true beginning of the Noldor's sorrow and the Flight from Aman.

Legacy Stats

House Founding Absolute
Tragedy Rating High
crown

Líder de los Sabios

Guided the Noldor from the shores of Cuiviénen through the Great Journey. Under his wisdom, the Noldor built Tirion upon Túna, becoming the most skilled and scholarly of all Elven kindreds in Aman.

favorite

Un Corazón Dividido

A life marked by profound loves: first for Míriel Serindë, then for Indis of the Vanyar. His devotion to his firstborn, Fëanor, was so total that he chose to follow him into exile at Formenos when the Silmarils were at stake.

El legado del Rey Fundador

Finwë remains the noble pillar of the Noldor. His death was not just a personal loss, but the catalyst for the rebellion and the darkening of Valinor. All the great houses—Fëanor, Fingolfin, and Finarfin—carry his name and the weight of his tragic sacrifice.
