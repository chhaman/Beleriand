Profile: Fourth Son of Fëanor

El Señor de las Riquezas: Caranthir

Señor de Thargelion • Elf (Noldor)

bolt

Key Fact

El Áspero

Known as Moryfinwë in Quenya, Caranthir was the most quick to anger of all the brothers. His stern nature defined his rule over the wild lands of the East.

"He was the harshest of the brothers and the most quick to anger."
shield

Alineación Ambivalente

Despite his rough exterior, Caranthir offered protection to the People of Haleth when they were besieged by Orcs. While he viewed them as lesser, his intervention saved their lineage from total destruction in Thargelion.

payments

Alianza con los Enanos

He established a controlled but lucrative trade route with the Dwarves of Nogrod and Belegost. His mastery over commerce made him the wealthiest of the Noldor princes, though the alliance was based on mutual profit rather than friendship.

history_edu

Memorable Acts

Defining moments of his legacy in Middle-earth

La Ruina de Thargelion

The invasion by the hosts of Morgoth that devastated his realm, leading to the desperate battle where he discovered the bravery of Haleth and her people.

The Oath Fulfilled

Bound by the Oath of Fëanor, Caranthir participated in the tragic kinslayings, driven by the inescapable fire of his father's vow and the pursuit of the Silmarils.

Master of the East

Fortifying the hills of Helevorn, he became the primary buffer between the North and the trade routes, consolidating elven power through economic dominance.
